# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Office managers, IT administrators and practice owners at small and mid-size businesses in and around Braunschweig. They arrive with a concrete need during working hours (a failed copier, an office move, a new hire's desk) and want to know quickly: is it in stock, what does it cost, and will this company install or fix it.

## Product Purpose

Eykotech is a local supplier of IT equipment, office furniture, printers and networking gear that also installs, delivers and repairs what it sells. The site must prove the company is real, stocked and competent, and let a visitor send an order request (cart, then email) or reach a person.

## Brand Personality

Dependable, plainspoken, corporate-calm. The look is the one the original build established: Sora headings, Inter body, the logo blue (#006cb1) on light blue-grey surfaces, rounded cards, photographic hero. Design work refines this system; it does not replace it.

## Anti-references

- AI-template tells: gradient text, emoji icons, drifting blur blobs, glass panels, bouncy animations, tracked-out monospace labels, em dashes in copy.
- Startup playfulness that undercuts a B2B supplier.
- Any redesign that discards the original author's visual language.

## Design Principles

1. Refine, do not replace. Keep existing fonts, tokens, component shapes and page structure.
2. Enterprise calm: solid surfaces, hairlines over glows, restrained shadows, consistent icons.
3. Say it with specifics: stock counts, hours, address, brands carried.
4. Reaching a person is always one step away.
5. Motion confirms an action, then gets out of the way. No bounce.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: visible focus on every control (including form fields), full keyboard operation, reduced-motion support, labelled icon buttons.
